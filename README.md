# A1 Construction - Brand Identity Assets

This repository contains the digital design assets for the corporate identity of **A1 Construction**, featuring the company banner/card back and the official business card mockup.

---

## 🏢 Company Profile

* **Company Name:** A1 Construction
* **Tagline:** BUILT ON TRUST, FOCUSED ON QUALITY.

---

## 📇 Asset Breakdown

The design showcases a two-part layout using a sophisticated corporate color scheme of deep navy blue and a textured light grey/off-white background.

### 1. Brand Banner / Card Back (Top Section)
* **Elements:** Features the central stylized lotus/overlapping geometric logo icon.
* **Text:** Includes the company name, corporate tagline, and a navy horizontal bar displaying the official website URL.

### 2. Business Card Layout (Bottom Section)
A classic split-color landscape business card layout:

* **Left Side (Light Grey Textured Canvas):**
  * Displays the core corporate logo and tagline.
* **Right Side (Deep Navy Blue Canvas):**
  * **Name:** NOLAN
  * **Title:** MANAGING DIRECTOR
  * **Contact Details:**
    * 📍 *Address:* 12, Vilachery Main, TPK
    * ✉️ *Email:* a1construction@gmail.com
    * 📞 *Phone:* +123-456-7890

---

## 🎨 Visual Specifications

* **Primary Palette:** * Deep Corporate Navy Blue (`#2C3E6B` approx.)
  * Soft Cream/Off-White (`#EAEAEA` approx.) with a subtle geometric arched texture layer.
* **Typography:** Clean, high-legibility geometric sans-serif fonts used across all branding titles and body copy.
